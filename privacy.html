Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Wardline Labs Ltd ("Wardline", "we", "us") provides an AI-assisted platform that helps SMBs continuously scan for and remediate vulnerabilities. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, work email, organization name, and role.
  • Infrastructure data you connect — configurations, dependencies, and endpoint metadata from the systems you link.
  • Usage data — how you interact with the product, used to improve reliability and features.
  • Technical data — device, browser, and log information collected to keep the service secure.

How we use your information

  • To scan for vulnerabilities and prioritize findings by risk.
  • To generate remediation guidance and compliance reports.
  • To operate, secure, maintain, and improve the platform.
  • To communicate about your account, updates, and support.

Data protection & security

Your scan data is yours. Each organization's data is isolated in its own workspace, encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not sell your data, and we do not use one organization's data to train models shared with another.

Scan data may reveal sensitive details about your infrastructure. You are responsible for having the appropriate authorization to scan the systems you connect to Wardline. We apply role-based access controls, audit logging, and least-privilege practices across our infrastructure.

AI processing

Vulnerability detection, risk ranking, and remediation guidance are performed by AI models running on our secured cloud infrastructure. Findings are decision-support, not a guarantee — your team is responsible for verifying and applying fixes.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers who help us operate the platform (such as cloud hosting and analytics), under contracts that require them to protect it, and where required by law.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email privacy@wardline.io or write to Wardline Labs Ltd, Data Protection Office.
